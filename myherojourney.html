MY HERO JOURNEY

An interactive multimedia exhibition exploring the archetypal hero's journey through personal transformation. This project combines narrative storytelling with immersive visual experiences to guide visitors through stages of growth, challenge, and self-discovery.

CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

The initial concept emerged from Joseph Campbell's monomyth theory, exploring how universal patterns of transformation can be made tangible through interactive design. This first phase focused on identifying the core emotional beats of the hero's journey.

INTERACTIVE PROTOTYPING

Moving beyond static displays, I developed responsive elements that react to visitor presence and engagement. The goal was to create a personalized journey where each visitor's interaction shapes their unique experience of transformation.

VISUAL DESIGN LANGUAGE

The visual elements were carefully crafted to evoke archetypal imagery while remaining accessible to contemporary audiences. Each panel represents a different stage of the journey, using color, typography, and imagery to guide emotional progression.

VISITOR IMPACT

The completed exhibition serves as both mirror and map for visitors, reflecting their own experiences while providing guidance for future growth. Feedback has shown that visitors leave with a deeper understanding of their own potential for transformation and change.
