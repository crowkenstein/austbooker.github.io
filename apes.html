How The World Went Ape$h!t

Feature-length VR documentary combining realtime characters with live-action footage, created in VR for Unity 6 URP/HDRP

Technical Art Leadership

Led Technical Art department under CG Supervisor at Mindshow, directing Unity 2023 URP to Unity 6 URP/HDRP upgrade while coordinating with 3D Modeling, Environment, and Rigging supervisors.

Realtime Lighting

Directed lighting implementation, creating systems to match realtime lighting with background plates for seamless integration between 3D ape characters and live-action footage.

Custom Shaders

Authored cross-compatible shaders between URP VR and HDRP pipelines, enabling real-time character animation that seamlessly transitioned between rendering pipelines while minimizing graphics hurdles.

C# Editor Tools

Developed custom C# editor tools for rapid asset conversion during pipeline transition. Collaborated with Art Supervisors to identify PBR differences between Maya and Unity, creating automated solutions for hundreds of assets.

Asset Pipeline

Worked with pipeline supervisor to resolve Shotgrid to Unity URP/HDRP compatibility issues, maintaining visual fidelity between Unity Rendering Pipelines and Maya RedShift.

Project Conclusion

Working remotely on this VR documentary, I successfully managed pipeline upgrades through constant communication between departments. The most rewarding part was keeping everyone aligned while switching rendering systems mid-production. I really enjoyed collaborating with the talented team at Mindshow throughout this transition.
