How The World Went Ape$h!t

Feature-length VR documentary combining realtime characters with live-action footage, created in VR for Unity 6 URP/HDRP

Technical Art Leadership

Led Technical Art department under CG Supervisor, directing pipeline upgrade from Unity 2023 URP to Unity 6 URP/HDRP while coordinating with 3D Modeling, Environment, and Rigging supervisors to maintain performance and visual fidelity.

Realtime Lighting

Directed lighting implementation for the project, creating and developing systems for matching realtime lighting to background plates. Achieved seamless integration between 3D ape characters and live-action footage through realtime lighting with a focus on optimization.

Custom Shaders

Authored and maintained cross-compatible shaders between URP VR and HDRP pipelines, enabling real-time character animation in Mindshow VR URP that seamlessly transitioned to HDRP rendering, minimizing graphics hurdles and expediting switching between rendering pipelines.

C# Editor Tools

Developed custom C# editor tools for rapid asset conversion during pipeline transition. Collaborated with Art Supervisors to identify PBR differences between Maya and Unity, creating automated solutions that ensured consistency across hundreds of assets.

Asset Pipeline

Worked with pipeline team to resolve Shotgrid to Unity URP/HDRP compatibility issues, maintaining visual fidelity parity between Unity HDRP and Maya RedShift renders.

Project Impact

This project strengthened my ability to balance innovation with production constraints across multiple rendering pipelines while bridging creative and technical teams remotely. It reinforced the value of cross-leadership collaboration, systematic thinking, and building tools that help artists work effectively during major technical transitions.
